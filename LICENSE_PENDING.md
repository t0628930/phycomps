# Noncommercial licensing decision pending

The authors authorized publication of their scripts and results. Their stated
preference is that commercial use requires a separate agreement. MIT, BSD and
GPL do not implement that restriction. No software license is granted by this file.

An unmodified PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 license is a candidate for review, not
an applied license. It includes explicit permissions for specified nonprofit,
educational and public institutions regardless of funding, and a patent grant.
Those provisions must be reviewed against the intended policy and ownership.
https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0

Confirm rights holders, third-party terms and the scope of code/results licensing
before release. A source-available noncommercial release must not be called
OSI-approved open source. Legal/institutional review is still needed.
